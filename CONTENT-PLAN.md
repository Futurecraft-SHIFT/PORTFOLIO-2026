# Alexandre Bersia — Digital Creative Director Portfolio

This is the working content map for the parallel portfolio. The published automotive/CUPRA portfolio remains unchanged.

## Narrative ownership rule

Each project has one primary home. It can reveal several layers inside that chapter, but it is not reused later as proof of a different capability.

## 00 — I DESIGN EXPERIENCES

Purpose: establish Alexandre as a designer of interactions and emotional outcomes, not a specialist in one medium.

Committed material:

- Industrial design and digital craft
- Scenography and spatial experience
- Ricard Pétanque as an embedded, playable cultural experience

Useful additions:

- Digital products or interfaces
- Interactive installations
- Collaborations involving physical and digital assets
- Product ecosystems with an especially strong user experience

Avoid here:

- Reusing Gramicci, ACG or CUPRA
- Positioning Ricard as a future-tech case study; its value is play, culture and personality

## 01 — I DESIGN STORIES

Purpose: prove the ability to create desire, culture, atmosphere and brand mythology.

Committed material:

- Nike / Objects Become Culture: campaign film plus the flower-jewelry portraits
- Gramicci: world-building, locations, culture, animated language and collectible assets
- CUPRA: cinematic direction, holographic emblem, ultra-wide heroes and monumental presentation

Embedded behavior:

- Gramicci unfolds directly in the landing-page scroll through six location states, moving terrain, editorial layers and collectible assets. The external project is a continuation, not a prerequisite.

Useful additions:

- A distinct lifestyle or fashion collaboration
- A title sequence, launch film or music-related story
- Character, costume or prop direction
- A campaign with a clearly different visual language from Nike, Gramicci and CUPRA

Avoid here:

- ACG, because its strongest value is system logic rather than visual campaign styling
- Reusing Gramicci later as a system-design example

## 02 — I DESIGN SYSTEMS

Purpose: demonstrate that creative direction includes information, process, interaction and repeatable quality — not only final images.

Committed material:

- ACG Barcelona: weather, 570 climate shelters, 3D map, product utility and field notes
- The six-stage creative-system diagram: Insight → Ideate → Generate → Model → Refine → Impact
- KIA Future Design R&D and post-digital creative-process leadership

Embedded behavior:

- ACG unfolds directly in the page from a live climate-shelter map into weather, utility and asset layers before transitioning into the wider creative-system diagram.

Useful additions:

- Clear evidence of team leadership, workshops or creative operations
- A before/after workflow transformation
- Custom AI or ComfyUI pipeline material
- A design system, content engine or tool used by other creators
- Metrics, scale or outcomes where they can be shared

Avoid here:

- Reusing Nike or Gramicci merely because they contain repeatable visual rules

## 03 — DESIGNING THE FUTURE

Purpose: show where Alexandre is going next, especially in relation to entertainment, real-time tools and emerging creative practice.

Committed structure:

- Three flexible content slots that can accept stills, video, 3D or interactive work
- Real-time worlds
- Generative assets
- New collaboration models

Priority gaps:

- Unreal Engine or real-time environment work
- Game-ready props, vehicles, characters, wearables or collaborative asset creation
- AI-native creative tools or workflows
- A digital twin, virtual production or interactive 3D project
- A forward-looking collaboration with a strong entertainment angle

Avoid here:

- Ricard Pétanque; it belongs to Experiences
- Generic futuristic imagery without a credible project or process behind it

## 04 — THE HUMAN IN THE SYSTEM

Purpose: preserve the personal, culturally engaged and hands-on creative identity that worked well in V2.4.

Committed material:

- Portrait and name treatment
- Barcelona collage
- Rally-culture image
- Personal fashion explorations
- Concise biography and career facts

Useful additions:

- A stronger creative-director biography for digital products and entertainment
- Selected collaborators, roles or recognizable brands if approved
- A downloadable résumé
- LinkedIn or another public professional profile

## 05 — LET’S DESIGN WHAT COMES NEXT

Purpose: end on possibility and make contact effortless.

Committed material:

- Large final statement
- Direct email link
- Return-to-top action

Useful additions:

- LinkedIn
- Résumé download
- Availability or preferred collaboration format

## Flexible build architecture

- The five narrative chapters remain fixed.
- Project details are data-driven in `script.js`.
- Every project can accept still images, natural-ratio artwork or video.
- Ricard is isolated in `games/ricard-petanque.html` and loaded only when played.
- The Future chapter uses replaceable slots, so new content will not require a page redesign.
- External live projects remain available as deep links while selected material is presented inside the portfolio.
