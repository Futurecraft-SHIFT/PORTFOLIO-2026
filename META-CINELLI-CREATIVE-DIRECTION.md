# META × CINELLI — Creative Direction and Strategy

Status: independent speculative portfolio concept. Not affiliated with or commissioned by Meta or Cinelli.

## Strategic role in the portfolio

The project sits between **Movement** and **Systems**. It demonstrates that a creative strategist can begin with a human behavior, define a product role, connect that role to Meta's ecosystem, build a modular campaign platform, and establish responsible-AI and measurement principles.

The story is not “technology added to glasses.” It is:

> A performance object lets athletes stay inside the moment while bringing other people into it.

Working platform: **MOTION, SHARED.**

## Mood-board analysis

### 1. The object is the interface

The strongest references make the eyewear the point where human presence, physical performance and technology meet. Tight product framing makes the camera understandable; human imagery proves why it matters. The new campaign alternates between full, uncropped product views and action context.

### 2. Two visual temperatures

The references divide into two useful worlds:

- Warm red/orange graphic energy communicates recording, momentum and cultural memory.
- Cool cobalt/cyan light communicates intelligence, connection and the Meta ecosystem.

The original direction combines them rather than reproducing either reference language. Acidic lime is used only as a performance and responsibility accent.

Palette:

- Graphite: `#05070B`
- Meta cobalt: `#1769FF`
- Signal cyan: `#22D8FF`
- Performance acid: `#B6FF2E`
- Capture orange: `#FF4B23`
- Warm paper: `#F2F0E9`

### 3. Movement is shown as a signal

Instead of copying the supplied concentric red bands, the portfolio uses asymmetrical connection fields, moving trace lines, nodes and an original three-dimensional signal path. These elements behave like information travelling through space rather than a decorative retro frame.

### 4. Athlete-first photography

Casting should feel contemporary, inclusive and credible. Sweat, real fabric, track texture and aerodynamic posture matter more than synthetic futurism. The product stays manufacturable and the athlete remains the subject; AI is a quiet layer, not a science-fiction spectacle.

### 5. Typography carries strategy

Large condensed statements deliver the creative platform. Small monospaced labels expose the system behind it: human tension, product behavior, channel role, safeguards and measurement. Text sits in the webpage rather than inside generated imagery so it remains accurate, accessible and adaptable.

## Locked product invariant

The Cinelli Aero Visor is not redesigned.

The following remain locked:

- shield-lens silhouette and proportions;
- white wraparound top bar and curve;
- nose geometry;
- ventilation rhythm;
- striped hinge accents;
- temple form;
- iridescent lens treatment;
- Cinelli identity and racing-memory cues.

The only product intervention is a compact camera integrated flush into the central top bar above the nose. The central position aligns more closely with the athlete's point of view, reduces lateral parallax, maintains visual balance, and limits the possibility of the frame entering the captured image. A small capture indicator sits beside it.

## Experience system

1. **SEE** — preserve a first-person sporting moment.
2. **UNDERSTAND** — use voice and contextual AI without introducing another screen.
3. **SHARE** — select the appropriate expression across Reels, Feed or Messaging.
4. **BELONG** — turn individual movement into conversation and community.

No autonomous sharing is implied. The athlete remains in control of capture, interpretation and publication.

## Campaign architecture

- **Hero film:** the moment stays in motion.
- **Product story:** a central camera, integrated without compromising the iconic silhouette.
- **Reels:** fast first-person action structured around rhythm, sound and movement.
- **Messaging:** meaningful private sharing with the people who matter.
- **Feed:** training chapters, route culture, progress and identity.
- **Creator toolkit:** repeatable prompts, shot grammar and modular graphic signals for athletes and communities.

## Responsible-AI layer

- A visible capture indicator makes recording legible to nearby people.
- Capture, interpretation and sharing require explicit athlete control.
- Claims should remain inside verified product capabilities.
- Accuracy, bias, safety and privacy reviews are part of campaign development.
- Sensitive contexts require stricter creative and product rules.

## Measurement framework

The portfolio presents a hypothetical framework, not invented results:

- Attention: view-through and completion.
- Utility: saves and product exploration.
- Connection: shares, messages and community response.
- Learning: creative signals and iteration velocity.

## Originality guardrails

- Do not reproduce the red contour-frame layout from the supplied advertisements.
- Do not repeat reference slogans or copy their typographic composition.
- Do not imitate Ray-Ban or Oakley frame geometry.
- Do not use the supplied people, poses or image crops as templates.
- Do not alter the Cinelli glasses silhouette.
- Use the official names only to identify the speculative collaboration; use original art direction for every campaign expression.
